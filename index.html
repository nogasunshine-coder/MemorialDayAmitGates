📖 Grammar Focus — Read First

Every sentence needs a verb

Look at these sentences Itamar said:

  • I get it now.
  • I see what you mean.
  • It's a lot.
  • It sounds really sad.
  • That's so sad.

In English, every sentence must have a verb. The verb tells us what is happening or gives information about the subject.

Some verbs show actions:
She goes to school.
Soldiers protect the country.
We talked about it at school.
Other verbs (BE: am/are/is) give information:
That's so sad.
I'm from Israel.
My keys are on the table.
Even if there is no action, you still need a verb to make a correct sentence!
✗ Incorrect: My phone on the table.  |  ✓ Correct: My phone is on the table.
✗ Incorrect: He really nice.  |  ✓ Correct: He is really nice.

Sometimes, people forget to add BE (am/are/is) because it isn't always needed in other languages. But in English, you must always include a verb!
Note: Some short phrases can stand without verbs: Good morning.   Thanks.   Bye for now.   Silence!
